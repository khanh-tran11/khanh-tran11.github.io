VeriHealth – Machine Learning Engineering and Research Fellow Built with IBM Bob

Sep 2026 – Present

IBM SkillsBuild AI Experiential Learning Lab · Remote

Why it exists. In Vietnam, my aunt saw a Facebook ad for a herbal product that promised to “thanh nhiệt, giải độc gan” (cool the body, detox the liver) and cure fatty liver in 30 days. She couldn’t tell if it was real, and doctors at overloaded hospitals don’t have time to explain. The official answer exists, but it’s buried in Ministry of Health PDFs written in medical language she doesn’t use. A search for “giải độc gan” never matches those clinical terms, so fact-checking first needs translation: folk words to medical concepts. That’s VeriHealth.

The why

Is this real??

The gap

Built with IBM Bob

I write the brain!

I write the plumbing.

The verdict

Every verdict has a citation!

What I did

  • Building a full-stack React + FastAPI app that verifies Vietnamese health claims against official sources, mapping folk phrases to clinical terms and returning cited verdicts via retrieval-augmented generation on IBM watsonx.
  • Built a data ingestion pipeline with BeautifulSoup and pdfplumber, loading 300+ posts and 50+ official documents into PostgreSQL + pgvector.
  • Training scikit-learn baselines and recurrent and Transformer models from scratch in PyTorch on 300 labeled claims.
  • Containerized services with Docker and set up continuous integration with pytest, using IBM Bob for code review. My rule with Bob: Bob writes the plumbing, I write the brain.
